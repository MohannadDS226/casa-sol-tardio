01 · The House

This is not a page. It is a house.

The website is structured like Casa itself: an opening threshold, a living archive of light, a screening room, a production floor, a reading room, and finally a private door.

Architecture · Memory · The Road Home

A house made for the slower hours.

Casa Sol Tardío is a cinematic architectural study shaped by Mediterranean light, memory, material and the quiet rituals of home.

Conceived in 2023 and completed in 2026, the project moves through the villa as a lived story rather than a sequence of isolated renders. Every room, still, film, production moment and printed page belongs to the same world.
